### A long-established tavern in Tenma, open all day from 11 AM. Daytime drinking is welcome!

Located in the Ten 5 area of the longest shopping street in Japan, the Tenjinbashi-suji Shopping Street, this long-established tavern has been serving customers for 50 years. With a nostalgic and rela...Read morexing atmosphere, it offers "affordable," "delicious," and "quick" meals made from fresh ingredients sourced from the market, making it a favorite among many. Both the food and drinks are wallet-friendly, featuring a wide variety from classic Dote-yaki to seafood and snacks.   
  
Don't miss the homemade potato salad!   
  
And for drinks, you can't go wrong with our unique Sakagura Highball, a refreshing mix of whiskey and lemon chuhai!
