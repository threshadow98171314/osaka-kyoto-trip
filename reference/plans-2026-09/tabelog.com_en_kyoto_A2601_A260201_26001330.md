### A beloved eel cuisine specialty shop in Kyogoku, cherished for over a century since the late Meiji era. Enjoy it in-house or take it to go!

Established in the Meiji era, Kyogoku Kaneyo is a long-standing eel specialty restaurant. We serve eel sourced mainly from Kyushu, expertly grilled to achieve a fluffy texture by our skilled chefs. Th...Read moree rich flavor of our secret sauce, passed down since our founding, enhances the taste even further.   
  
One of our popular dishes is the Kinshi-don, featuring eel rice topped with Kyoto-style dashi omelet. The retro interior, unchanged since our establishment, is also a highlight.   
  
Additionally, we host monthly performances in our second-floor tatami room.   
  
Takeout is also available.
