### A long-established Sukiyaki specialty restaurant founded in 1873, offering the finest quality black wagyu beef with a unique aging technique.

Located an 8-minute walk from Sanjo Station on the Keihan Line, Mishimatei is a long-established sukiyaki specialty restaurant nestled in the Sanjo Teramachi Kyogoku shopping district. The charming tr...Read moreaditional machiya-style building, constructed in the early Meiji period, adds to the ambiance.   
  
The chef personally selects the finest black wagyu beef, which is aged using a unique method to enhance its flavor and depth, ensuring it is served at its best. In addition to sukiyaki, you can also enjoy it in dishes like mizutaki and oil hot pot. The skilled waitstaff expertly prepare these dishes right at your table, providing a delightful dining experience.
