### Over 150 selected local sake from Hyogo! Enjoy them in a relaxed atmosphere along with seasonal sake dishes!

We offer over 150 types of local sake from Hyogo, including seasonal selections. Enjoy a diverse lineup of dishes that go beyond typical izakaya fare, such as our recommended "Assorted Sashimi (5 type...Read mores)", "Grilled Pork with Egg-filled Potato Salad", and "Banshu Oden (winter only)". Located near Sannomiya Station, you can relax whether you're dining solo or in a group. Private reservations are also available. Even if we show as fully booked, we have phone reservation slots.
