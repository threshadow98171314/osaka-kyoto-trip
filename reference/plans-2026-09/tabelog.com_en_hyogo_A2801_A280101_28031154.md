### [2-minute walk from Sannomiya Station] A rich demi-glace sauce connecting history and tradition. Enjoy heartwarming moments at this long-established Western-style restaurant, celebrating 74 years of culinary excellence.

Our restaurant proudly serves a rich demi-glace sauce that has been passed down for many years. Enjoy our immensely popular "Hearty Beef Cutlet," nostalgic "Omelette Rice," shrimp fried to perfection ...Read morewith generous homemade tartar sauce, and sizzling "Spaghetti Italian" served hot on a plate. Each dish is crafted with care and attention to detail.   
The interior offers a relaxed atmosphere, welcoming everyone from solo diners to groups. Come and enjoy delicious Western cuisine while creating wonderful memories!
