### Directly connected to Kyoto Station, enjoy Kyoto vegetables tempura and carefully selected seasonal sake. The Yuki course is highly recommended!

Located directly at Kyoto Station, Tempura Restaurant "Tenpura-dokoro Kyorin-sen" is operated by the renowned Michelin two-star restaurant "Sansou Kyoyamato." Using seasonal Kyoto vegetables, we exper...Read moretly blend white sesame oil and pure sesame oil. We offer different courses for lunch and dinner, with the "Yuki Course" being particularly recommended. For the course meal's finale, you can choose between "Kakiage Donburi" or "Chirimen Jako Rice." Enjoy a luxurious moment in a serene Japanese atmosphere.

Food Using a blend of two carefully selected oils and premium Kyoto vegetables.

In the Kanto region, tempura is said to primarily use sesame oil, while in Kansai, white sesame oil is the standard. At Kyobun-sen, we blend premium "Shiro-mon oil" with the finest "Taihaku sesame oil" in a unique ratio. The highly refined and light "Shiro-mon oil" ensures no heartburn, while the aromatic and rich "Taihaku sesame oil" adds depth. After much experimentation, we've achieved a perfect balance. We also source our tempura ingredients from the same suppliers as our main store, featuring carefully selected Kyoto vegetables.
