Food Crispy, fragrant, and tender! Fukuoka's specialty "Kawa-yaki" now in Kyoto.

Our signature dish, "Meidai Torikawa," was born in 1968 at a long-established yakitori restaurant. The founder of "Hakata Kaway" later developed a unique method for preparing it.
The process involves wrapping chicken skin, grilling it to render the fat, and letting it rest for a day, which is repeated over six days. After the first and fourth grilling, the chicken is marinated in different sauces.
This meticulous preparation results in a crispy, fragrant exterior and a tender, juicy interior with a distinctive flavor. Experience this beloved taste of Fukuoka right here in Kyoto!
